FAQ

My shots aren’t being saved.
Make sure microphone access is on — shots are found by the sound of impact. In a very noisy place, or if the phone is far from the ball, some shots may be missed. You can save a missed shot with the “Save now” button (or your phone’s volume button, on supported devices).

Do my records move to a new phone?
Records live only on your phone and don’t move automatically. Save the videos you want to keep to your photo library with “Save to Photos”.

What happens if I delete the app?
All videos and records inside the app are deleted. Videos and photos you saved to your photo library stay.

Can I turn off personalized ads?
Yes. See section 4 of the Privacy Policy.
