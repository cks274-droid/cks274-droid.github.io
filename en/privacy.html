Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Summary
  • Golf Notes has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run any server for the app.
  • Your practice videos, photos, voice memos, notes and practice records are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.
  • Voice memos are transcribed on your device; recordings are not sent to a server.
  • Ads in the app are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google processes device information such as your advertising identifier.

This policy explains how the Golf Notes app (the “app”) handles information. The developer of Golf Notes (“we”, “us”) is the controller for the limited information described in section 1.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect or store personal information through the app. The app has no account system and does not send your records to us.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and message so we can reply. We delete them once your request is handled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays in the app’s storage on your device. We cannot access it.

WhatShot videos (a few seconds around each shot) and thumbnails, photos of shot frames and comparisons, voice memo recordings and their text, notes, and practice records such as date, place name, club and shot count
How longUntil you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes it.
BackupsDepending on your phone’s backup settings (iCloud, Google backup), some records may be included in your own backup. Apple or Google stores that backup in your account; we cannot access it. On iOS, shot video files are excluded from backup.

Videos and photos you choose to save with “Save to Photos” are copied to your photo library and are then managed by your device and photo app.

3. Permissions the app uses

CameraRecording your swing
MicrophoneRecording voice memos and video sound, and detecting the sound of impact to save shots automatically
Speech recognitionTurning voice memos into text, on your device only
Photos (add only)Saving videos and photos you choose to your photo library
App Tracking (iOS)Asking whether ads may be personalized

Each permission is requested when you first use the related feature. If you decline, everything else still works, and you can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob banner ads on some screens (not on the recording, shot, or comparison screens). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID)
  • IP address, device model, operating system and language, and approximate location derived from IP
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks

Google receives this information directly from your device; we do not receive information that identifies you. Your practice videos, photos, audio, notes and records are never used for ads or sent to Google.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are shown, the app asks for your consent using Google’s consent form. You can refuse or choose which purposes to allow.
  • iOS: choose “Ask App Not to Track” when asked, or turn tracking off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy (or Google) › Ads.

If you opt out, every feature still works; you may see non-personalized ads.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Personalized ads and the related use of identifiers: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free app.
  • Replying to your emails: our legitimate interest in answering your request.

6. International transfers

Advertising information is processed by Google LLC and its affiliates, which may process it in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google uses safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share your app records with anyone. The advertising information in section 4 goes directly from your device to Google. Under some U.S. state laws, personalized advertising can count as “sharing”; you can opt out using the device settings in section 4.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Your rights

All app records are on your device, so you can view, edit and delete them in the app, and deleting the app removes them. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. For information Google processes for ads, you can also use Google’s tools linked above. Contact us at the address below and we will respond without undue delay. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority.
